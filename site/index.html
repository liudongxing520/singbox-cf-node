📢 Visitor Notice & Flying Guidelines

Visitors are always welcome! For safety, all guests must report to the flight line/pits upon arrival. Flying operates strictly under Model Aeronautical Association of Australia (MAAA) safety rules.

Club Overview

Flying Schedule

  • Weekends: Saturday & Sunday, 8:30 AM – Sunset
  • Mid-Week: Wednesday mornings (Weather permitting)
  • Instruction: Dual-control flight training available on weekend mornings
  • IC Engines: Subject to local noise time windows

Field & Facilities

  • Well-maintained grass runway
  • Covered pit areas and assembly tables
  • Safety boundary netting for spectators
  • Dedicated frequency & transmitter safety area
  • On-site parking and club shelter

Join QMAC

  • MAAA Insurance: Comprehensive coverage provided
  • Flight Training: Free instructor assistance for novices
  • All Aircraft Welcome: Fixed-wing, Gliders, Helis & Drones
  • Categories: Senior, Junior, and Family Memberships
